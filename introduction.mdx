The **Raiz do Bem API** is the transactional core of the Raiz do Bem platform — a **FIAP Challenge** project built in partnership with the **Turma do Bem NGO**, the world's largest specialized volunteering network focused on the oral health of people in social vulnerability. The API replaces manual and decentralized processes with a modern, scalable, and auditable backend built on **Java 21** and **Quarkus 3.34.5**, centralizing beneficiary management, help-request triage, volunteer dentist coordination, geographic appointment matching, and internal governance under a single enterprise-grade RESTful service.

## What Is Raiz do Bem?

The Turma do Bem NGO operates two flagship programs that the Raiz do Bem API directly supports:

- **Dentista do Bem** — Connects low-income young people in social vulnerability with volunteer dentists for free dental treatment.
- **Apolônias do Bem** — Provides free dental care specifically for women who are survivors of domestic violence.

The **Raiz do Bem API** acts as the orchestration engine for both programs, handling every step of the lifecycle from the initial public help request to the final closure of a dental appointment.

## What the API Does

The API centralizes five major operational domains:

- **Triage and Help Requests** — Accepts public solicitation submissions (`POST /pedido-ajuda`), validates eligibility automatically, and drives an approval workflow through a `StatusPedido` state machine (`ANALISE → APROVADO / REJEITADO`).
- **Beneficiary Management** — Converts approved requests into active beneficiaries linked to one of the social programs, with full CRUD and CSV export capabilities.
- **Volunteer Dentist Network** — Manages dentist registration, dental specialties, availability flags, and professional addresses used in geographic matching.
- **Appointment Orchestration and Geographic Match** — When an appointment is opened without a manually assigned dentist, `AtendimentoMatchService` calls the **Google Maps Routes API** (`computeRouteMatrix`) to find the available dentist closest in real road distance to the beneficiary's residential address.
- **Collaborator Governance** — Controls internal NGO staff via JWT-authenticated sessions and role-based access control (`ADMIN` and `COLABORADOR`), with CSV report exports for operational auditing.

## Architecture at a Glance

The application follows a strict **Layered Architecture** to guarantee high cohesion, low coupling, and a clean separation between the HTTP contract layer (DTOs) and the persistence model (JPA Entities):

```text
Consumers / Front-End
        │  HTTPS / JSON (Bearer JWT)
        ▼
Resource Layer  (JAX-RS, @RequestScoped)
        │  DTOs (Request / Response)
        ▼
Service Layer   (Business rules, @ApplicationScoped)
     ┌──┴──┐
     ▼     ▼
Repository     External Clients
(Panache ORM)  (ViaCEP · Google Maps)
     │
     ▼
Oracle Database
```

All unhandled exceptions are intercepted by `ExceptionsMapperGlobal` and serialized into a uniform `ErroResponse` JSON payload — no stack traces ever leak to consumers.
